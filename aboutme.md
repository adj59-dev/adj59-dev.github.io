My name is Alex Johnson. I have a background in experimental condensed matter physics, with a focus on two-dimensional semiconductors. After receiving my PhD, I moved into semiconductor manufacturing, where I specialize in electron-beam lithography.

I started this page while between roles in 2025, as my partner and I worked through the two-body problem. During that time, I decided to use my newfound free time to study quantum computation and quantum information, a field I had seriously considered pursuing for my PhD, but ultimately did not.

Since then, I’ve jumped between a variety of topics that have caught my interest. I post my notes and solutions here primarily as a way of keeping track of what I’m learning, but also in case they prove useful to someone else.
